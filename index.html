03 / Notes to keep

Good design starts with clarity.

  • Make room to read. Consistent line height and generous spacing give content room to breathe.
  • Help people find their way. Visible navigation and clear hover and focus states make links easier to use.
  • Design for different screens. Flexible layouts let the same content work on phones and desktops.
